# ACP GRS Accelerator for Axion - D0: Discovery

> **Gate**: D0 - Discovery | **Status**: Draft | **Epic**: EACPP-1443

## Problem Statement

The current GRS workflow, Axion's role in it, and the specific problem ACP onboarding should address have not yet been established. Discovery needs stakeholder evidence before this spec can make claims about current pain or constraints.

## Opportunity

If the GRS stakeholders confirm a fit, onboarding the Axion use case to ACP may provide governed access to ACP capabilities. The expected value and target outcome remain to be defined with the use-case owners.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Key Concern |
|---|---|---|
| GRS use-case owner - to confirm | Business owner | Desired outcome, scope, and measurable value |
| Axion product or service owner - to confirm | System owner | Axion capabilities, integration constraints, and support model |
| ACP Product Team | Platform onboarding | Capability fit, governance baseline, and dependencies |

## Scope

### In Scope

- Establish what GRS and Axion refer to in this use case and identify accountable stakeholders.
- Understand the current workflow, the problem to solve, and the desired business outcome.
- Confirm ACP capability requirements with stakeholders against the capability map.
- Define the use-case boundary, dependencies, constraints, and measurable success criteria.

### Out of Scope

- Selecting an architecture, API contract, or implementation approach before D1/D2.
- Committing to a delivery date or capability not confirmed with the stakeholders.
